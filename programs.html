Sec. 2: Uganda, Byerima Parish

The Foundations of Life Board approved Stage 1 on October 1, 2026. Stage 1 is a diagnostic and site assessment: observation on the ground, soil and land condition assessments, documentation of baseline conditions, and identification of the micro-pockets where restoration can begin. No funds move at this stage and no binding commitments are made. Field work begins once an implementing partner agreement with Green Youth Movement is signed. The output is a site report that informs whether, and how, the work proceeds, and that report returns to the Board for a decision on the next stage.

Sec. 3: Program Areas

Regenerative Agriculture Education

Teaching the practices of regenerative agriculture directly, and transferring that knowledge freely rather than holding it inside any single operation or geography.

Soil & Land Restoration Training

Hands-on training in rebuilding degraded soil and land health, treating restoration as a skill to be taught and practiced rather than a service performed for a fee.

Land Remediation Projects

Projects aimed at returning degraded and contaminated land to productive, living use, reversing the damage synthetic dependency leaves behind.

Food Sovereignty Projects

Projects that help communities grow their own food on living soil, so that access to food does not depend on synthetic supply chains they cannot control.

Desertification Reversal Projects

Projects working to reverse desertification on degraded arid land, rebuilding the biological systems that allow it to support life again.

Sec. 4: How Programs Work

Every program moves through staged decisions, and written consent from the landholder and the community comes before any field work. Foundations of Life plans, designs, tests, and provides technical oversight. Local, in-country contractors do the physical work, so the skills and knowledge stay where the work happens and the community does not need Foundations of Life once the work is done.
