Sec. 1: Founder

Forty years in living soil, and counting.

Josh Blair has worked in living soil for more than forty years, and for twenty-nine of them that work has included commercial cultivation, caring for land and crops at production scale. The two are one body of experience: the long education only living soil gives, and the daily discipline of keeping land and crops healthy when a harvest depends on it. Alongside that, he worked as a subcontractor on Intel semiconductor fabrication floors, two disciplines held at once rather than one giving way to the other.

That dual background shaped an unusual way of seeing soil. A fabrication floor tolerates no guesswork, every variable measured, controlled, and accounted for, and Josh has brought that same expectation to the ground, treating a living biological system with the same precision and rigor as a controlled manufacturing environment rather than as a substrate to be dosed.

Sec. 2: One Lens

Forty years in living soil taught him to see the connections everywhere: how what lives beneath the surface connects to what grows above it, how contaminated land connects to contaminated food, how synthetic dependency in one place reverberates through water systems and communities and generations.

The precision and the connections became one lens.

Sec. 3: Why We Exist

The mission is simple, and it is everything.

We exist for the soil. Not for governments, not for borders, not for markets. For the living systems beneath our feet that every form of life on this planet depends on. We restore degraded and contaminated land. We break synthetic dependency in food production. We transfer the knowledge to do this freely, to anyone, anywhere, because soil does not recognize the lines we draw on maps. Our commitment is to the land itself, and to every generation that will live or die by what we do with it now.

Sec. 4: The Board

Foundations of Life is governed by a board of directors. The founding directors and officers are:

Chair and President, Legal seat

Diogo Busse

Diogo Busse is a Brazilian attorney, legal scholar, and cultural producer with extensive experience in regulatory law, public policy, civil rights, and the legal development of innovative initiatives addressing complex social challenges.

He holds a Master's degree in Social Relations Law from the Federal University of Paraná (UFPR) and a Legal Law Master (LL.M.) qualification from Ibmec. Throughout his career, he has worked at the intersection of law, science, public health, and social transformation, contributing to pioneering legal initiatives and regulatory discussions in Brazil.

His professional experience includes legal advocacy, institutional governance, nonprofit development, and the advancement of regulatory frameworks for emerging technologies and innovative approaches to health and well-being.

As Legal Director and a member of the Board of Directors of Foundations of Life, Diogo contributes legal and strategic guidance to support the organization's governance, institutional integrity, and international development.

He believes that law should serve as an instrument for protecting life, promoting social justice, and enabling meaningful transformation. His commitment to Foundations of Life reflects a broader conviction that environmental restoration, human dignity, and collective well-being are deeply interconnected.

Secretary, Policy and Legislative seat

Tom Lauerman

Farmer Tom Lauerman is an organic agricultural authority, federal educator, and recognized subject matter expert in cannabis and hemp cultivation. As the founder of Farmer Tom Organics, he bridges the gap between agricultural operations and public sector governance, working directly alongside federal agencies to develop compliant Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and advance national safety mandates.

A pioneer in organic medical production and processing, Tom's government work includes opening his research facility to federal scientists and providing direct training to researchers from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). His contributions led to landmark research published by the CDC in 2017. Beyond his work in federal education and safety compliance, Tom serves as a trusted consultant and expert witness in litigation involving hemp and cannabis regulation, agricultural compliance, and industry standards.

Treasurer, Community Health seat

Dr. Chris D. Meletis, ND

Dr. Chris Meletis is an educator, international author, and lecturer whose mission is “Changing the World’s Health One Person at a Time.” He has written 18 books and more than 200 scientific articles for publications including Natural Health, Alternative and Complementary Therapies, Townsend Letter for Doctors and Patients, Life Extension, NDNR, and Natural Pharmacy, with additional papers indexed in PubMed.

For seven years, Dr. Meletis served as Dean of Naturopathic Medicine and Chief Medical Officer at the National College of Naturopathic Medicine (now the National University of Natural Medicine). The American Association of Naturopathic Physicians named him its 2003 Physician of the Year. Committed to caring for the underserved, he spearheaded the creation of 16 free natural medicine clinics across the Portland, Oregon, metropolitan area.

Dr. Meletis has practiced in Oregon for more than 34 years as a primary care physician with a functional medicine approach. His clinical work centers on the GI microbiome, mitochondrial dysfunction, nutritional biochemistry, and botanical medicine.
