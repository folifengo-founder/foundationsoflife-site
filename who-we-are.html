Sec. 1: Founder

Forty years in living soil, and counting.

Josh Blair has worked in living soil for more than forty years, and for twenty-nine of them that work has included commercial cultivation, caring for land and crops at production scale. The two are one body of experience: the long education only living soil gives, and the daily discipline of keeping land and crops healthy when a harvest depends on it. Alongside that, he worked as a subcontractor on Intel semiconductor fabrication floors, two disciplines held at once rather than one giving way to the other.

That dual background shaped an unusual way of seeing soil. A fabrication floor tolerates no guesswork, every variable measured, controlled, and accounted for, and Blair brought that same expectation to the ground, treating a living biological system with the same precision and rigor as a controlled manufacturing environment rather than as a substrate to be dosed. Foundations of Life is where that thinking gets applied beyond any single crop or company, to the broader work of restoring soil, water, and food systems wherever synthetic dependency has taken their place.
