Sec. 1: Founder

Forty years in living soil, and counting.

Josh Blair has worked in living soil for more than forty years, and for twenty-nine of them that work has included commercial cultivation, caring for land and crops at production scale. The two are one body of experience: the long education only living soil gives, and the daily discipline of keeping land and crops healthy when a harvest depends on it. Alongside that, he worked as a subcontractor on Intel semiconductor fabrication floors, two disciplines held at once rather than one giving way to the other.

That dual background shaped an unusual way of seeing soil. A fabrication floor tolerates no guesswork, every variable measured, controlled, and accounted for, and Josh has brought that same expectation to the ground, treating a living biological system with the same precision and rigor as a controlled manufacturing environment rather than as a substrate to be dosed.

Sec. 2: One Lens

Forty years in living soil taught him to see the connections everywhere: how what lives beneath the surface connects to what grows above it, how contaminated land connects to contaminated food, how synthetic dependency in one place reverberates through water systems and communities and generations.

The precision and the connections became one lens.

Sec. 3: Why We Exist

The mission is simple, and it is everything.

We exist for the soil. Not for governments, not for borders, not for markets. For the living systems beneath our feet that every form of life on this planet depends on. We restore degraded and contaminated land. We break synthetic dependency in food production. We transfer the knowledge to do this freely, to anyone, anywhere, because soil does not recognize the lines we draw on maps. Our commitment is to the land itself, and to every generation that will live or die by what we do with it now.
