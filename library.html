Library

Open knowledge, published as it's ready.

The Library holds framework-level protocol documents that Foundations of Life publishes openly, because the knowledge required to reverse degraded ground belongs to everyone. More will be added as they are prepared for public release.

Sec. 2 — Framework Documents

FoL-GDR-001

Global Desertification Reversal

The framework-level protocol for reversing desertification: outward-expansion sequencing, vertical stratification, plant selection, and an honest accounting of what's proven versus still theory.

FoL-VSM-001

Vertical Stratification Mulching

The layered soil-building structure at the core of the work: a woody base, an active soil layer, a stratifying mulch surface, and living cover, built from whatever is locally available.

FoL-HMM-001

Heavy Metal Mitigation

Proven results and honest limitations for remediating contaminated ground, and the field-scale mechanism used where a contained approach doesn't apply.
